The Philosophy

Style Lives Higher: The Architecture of Modern Menswear.

Founded on the belief that everyday clothing should possess the structural rigor of architecture and the sensory ease of loungewear, Cloth City creates garments that seamlessly bridge high-level boardroom meetings, intercontinental travel, and relaxed leisure.

Each silhouette in this capsule — from our signature double-knit mocha tracksuit to the hand-burnished Tuscan calfskin boots — is sampled, wear-tested, and tailored to provide an effortless drape that retains its clean geometry wash after wash.

Uncompromised Fibers

Giza Egyptian cotton poplin, Italian calfskin suedes, and thermal micro-twills.

Ergonomic Tailoring

Concealed stretch gussets, dual-way Excella zippers, and contoured waistbands.

The Cloth City Guarantee

If the drape or sizing isn't completely true to your silhouette, exchanges are on us within 30 days.
